# Exercise pictures

The pictures in this folder come from **Free Exercise DB** by yuhonas:
https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db

They are released into the public domain under the Unlicense, so they can
be copied and used freely. Only the exercises used by Family Hub's
built-in workout routines are included here (two frames each: start and
end position). Thank you to the project and its contributors.
